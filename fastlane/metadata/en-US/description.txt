CMTE Lori (Commander Lori) is a gamified study app for the Brazilian
ANAC Private Pilot License (PPL) written exam. Bite-sized lessons,
daily streaks, weekly leagues and a lot of aviation — built like
Duolingo, made for Brazilian student pilots.

WHY IT WORKS
• 5-minute lessons focused on what's actually on the ANAC exam
• Spaced repetition (SRS): you revisit what you're forgetting, not
  what you already know
• Daily streak to build the habit (the same trick that taught you a
  second language)
• Full mock exam in the official board format: 60 questions, 3 hours,
  70% per subject

CONTENT
7 complete subjects:
• Air Traffic Regulations
• Aviation Meteorology
• Air Navigation
• Theory of Flight
• Technical Knowledge
• Communications (ICAO phonetic alphabet included)
• Aircraft (image quiz of classic GA airplanes)

GAMIFICATION
• Hearts: lose them on wrong answers, regenerate over time
• Gems: earn studying, spend on mascot outfits
• Outfits: 12+ looks for Lori (Red Baron, Top Gun, Pro Gold…)
• Leagues: Bronze → Diamond, weekly promotions for the top 10
• Shop: daily roulette + weekly jackpot
• Achievements: 30+ badges to unlock

CMTE LORI PRO (optional)
• Unlimited hearts
• Extra mock exams + per-subject drills
• Advanced stats (accuracy by topic, weekly trend)
• Ad-free
• Priority support
• Pro Gold outfit
• 7 days free, cancel anytime

COMMUNITY
• Gallery: post photos of your flight or aircraft, like others
• Friends: follow classmates, watch their progress
• Schools: find ANAC-certified aviation schools near you

ABOUT THE BRAND
"Commander Lori" (the duck mascot) is not affiliated with ANAC and
doesn't represent any government body. Content is supplemental — read
the official ANAC materials too.

PRIVACY
We don't ask for your location. We don't use IDFA. We don't sell your
data. Full policy: https://capitaolori.com/privacy

Note: lesson content is in Brazilian Portuguese. UI is available in
English, Portuguese and Spanish.
